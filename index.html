預約系統使用指南
小泰空為「全自助預約」制 歡迎使用線上預約系統，24/7 隨意選擇老師與時段，預約超方便！以下是簡單的操作說明，幫助您輕鬆完成預約。 📅 每個月 25 日 13:00 開放下個月的預約，最晚須提早 1 小時才能預約
📅如何進行預約
Step 1 進入線上預約系統 ▾
前往 小泰空 LINE 官方帳號
點選選單左下角「點我預約」連結進入預約系統
💡
進入預約系統後，請先詳閱「注意事項」
Step 2 點選服務項目與服務人員 ▾
詳閱系統「注意事項」區塊後下滑，點選想預約的地點與時長方案
選擇「老師」或「 🔀 不指定」
點選「加入預約」
📍
服務據點
在服務選項的部分，請留意所選擇的據點「G18 南京三民」或「G14/R11中山」
🔖
系統金額以原價呈現
若符合條件，服務結束後，結算立享優惠價
🧑‍🤝‍🧑
多人同行
先於螢幕底端點擊「＋」調整人數，再依上述步驟加入多項服務
Step 3 選擇預約時段 ▾
選擇想要預約的日期與時段
點選「確認預約」
⚠️
請仔細確認日期
系統會直接跳至未來最早可預約的日期,敬請留意!
🧑‍🤝‍🧑
多人同行
請先勾選「需要同時進行」,顯示時間即為多位老師可以的交集時間
🔺
請不要再敲小編
時段及老師以系統為主,若無法預約即為已滿,可以嘗試選擇較短方案,看有無剩餘空檔
Step 4 加入您的號碼註冊／登入 ▾
選取國碼，填入號碼後，點選「登入／註冊」
資料填寫及簡訊驗證，後點擊「完成驗證」
🌏
註冊之電話號碼支援各國國碼，並非僅限於台灣
Step 5 確認並送出預約資訊 ▾
敬請再次仔細確認預約資訊
如需修改，請點擊「修改預約內容」進行調整
勾選「我同意」，點擊「確認預約」送出
🔖
系統金額以原價呈現
若符合條件,服務結束後,結算立享優惠價
Step 6 🟡 預約待確認 ▾
送出預約後,系統會傳送一則「預約待確認」訊息至 Line 官方帳號
🔺
預約尚未成功喲
請稍待我們確認預約
👪
須付訂金:3 人(含)以上同行
訂金為「總預約服務原價之 50%」,請與小編聯繫。我們收到訂金後,方能確認預約
Step 7 🟢 預約完成 ▾
我們確認預約後，系統會傳送「預約完成」訊息至 Line 官方帳號
請再次確認地點、日期、時間，以免白跑一趟
⚠️
時間可能微調
為確保老師服務品質,預約成功時間有可能有 15 分鐘內的微調
Step 8 準備享受放鬆時光 ▾
24 小時前，系統會傳送「預約提醒」訊息至 Line 官方帳號，敬請詳閱黃底提醒事項
預約時段將為您保留，請準時前來，小泰空期待您的蒞臨！
❌
取消政策
24 小時前請於系統自行取消，24 小時內聯繫小編取消
⏳
如果遲到怎麼辦？
若遲到超過 10 分鐘，服務將酌予縮短，以免影響後續賓客之服務。敬請準時抵達。遲到超過 40 分鐘且無法聯繫上，則視為No show，請注意我們有拒絕往來條款。請輸入提醒內容
⛔
拒絕往來條款
No show 或 1 小時內臨時取消達 1 次，視同不尊重老師時間與其他賓客權益,恕不再接受預約
🔍查看預約狀態
點擊此處進入預約系統「會員中心」>「我的紀錄」查看預約狀態。
或從 Line 官方帳號進入:
  • 前往 小泰空 Line 官方帳號
  • 點選選單左下角「點我預約」連結進入預約系統
  • 點擊左上角之「☰」圖樣 >「會員中心」>「我的紀錄」
❌取消政策與修改預約
取消政策
  • 24 小時前可於系統自行取消
  • 24 小時內請聯繫小編取消
🙏
為了其他賓客之權益，如果無法前來或有變卦，請盡早聯繫小編
⛔
拒絕往來條款
No show （遲到超過40分鐘且聯繫不上）或 1 小時內臨時取消達 1 次，視同不尊重老師時間與其他賓客權益，恕不再接受預約
修改預約
  • 24 小時前：請於系統自行取消，後再自行重新預約
  • 24 小時內：請聯繫小編，以當日預約情況為主，不保證能順利更動
BAAN NUAD THAI 24/7 self-service booking system Welcome! Enjoy 24/7 access to our booking system.

Although the interface is only in Chinese, we have prepared these detailed English instructions to ensure you can complete your reservation without any trouble.

Please refer to our English FAQ before booking to ensure you have all the information you need.

📅 Next month's slots open on the 25th at 13:00; please ensure you book at least 1 hour before your desired time.
📅How to Book
Step 1 Enter the booking system ▾
Open our official LINE account
Tap the orange "點我預約 (Book Now)" in the lower-left of the menu to enter the booking system
💡
Before you start, you'll see a '注意事項(Notice)' section in Chinese. Please check our English FAQ for a full explanation of these points.
Step 2 Choose service & specialists ▾
Scroll down & select your preferred location & duration. Look for the MRT station codes to choose your location: G18 南京三民Nanjing Sanmin or G14/R11 中山 Zhongshan.
Select a specific specialist or the first symble "🔀 不指定" as "No Preference"
Click the blue '加入預約(Add to Booking)' button at the very bottom to proceed with your reservation.
📍
We have 2 locations
In the Service section, look for the MRT station codes to choose your location: G18 南京三民Nanjing Sanmin or G14/R11 中山 Zhongshan.
🔖
The system displays original prices.
The system displays the original price. Eligible guests will automatically receive the discounted rate at checkout.
🧑‍🤝‍🧑
Group bookings:
"預約1位" at the buttom means reserve for a single spot. Tap "＋" to adjust the number of guests, then add services as above.
Step 3 Choose date & time ▾
Choose your date and time
Then, click '下一步 確認預約(Next: Confirm Booking)' located at the bottom right.
⚠️
Double-check the date.
The system jumps to the earliest available date — please confirm before submitting.
🧑‍🤝‍🧑
Group bookings:
Tick "需要同時進行 (Simultaneous)" — displayed times will show overlapping availability for all therapists.
🔺
Please don't message us to squeeze you in.
Slots and specialists are managed via the system. If unavailable, it's fully booked — try a shorter session for remaining slots.
Step 4 Sign up / log in with your phone ▾
Select your country code, enter your number, and tap "登入／註冊(Log in / Sign up)"
Fill in your details, complete SMS verification, then tap "完成驗證(Complete Verification)"

Note: "驗證碼" means "SMS varification code"
,and "密碼" means "password"
, and "確認密碼" means "comdfirm password"
🌏
International phone numbers are supported — not limited to Taiwan.
Step 5 Confirm & submit booking ▾
Carefully review all booking details
To make changes, tap "修改預約內容(Edit Booking)"
Tick "我同意(I Agree)" and tap the blue button "確認預約(Confirm Booking)" to submit
🔖
The system displays original prices.
The system displays the original price. Eligible guests will automatically receive the discounted rate at checkout.
Step 6 🟡 Booking Pending ▾
After submission, you'll receive a "預約待確認(Booking Pending)" message on Line with a yellow header.
🔺
Your booking is not yet confirmed.
Please wait for our confirmation.
👪
Deposit required for groups of 3+:
50% of the total original price. Please contact us via LINE— your booking will only be confirmed after the deposit is received.
Step 7 🟢 Booking Confirmed ▾
Once we confirm, you'll receive a "預約完成(Booking Confirmed)" message on Line with a green header.
Please double-check the location, date and time to avoid a wasted trip
⚠️
Time may be slightly adjusted.
To ensure service quality, the confirmed time may differ by up to 15 minutes from your requested time.
Step 8 Get ready to relax ▾
24 hours before, you'll receive a "Booking Reminder" on Line
Your slot is reserved. Please arrive on time — we look forward to welcoming you!
❌
Cancellation Policy
Cancel via the system at least 24 hours in advance. Within 24 hours, please contact us on LINE to cancel.
⏳
What happens if I am late?
Arriving 10+ minutes late will shorten your session to avoid impacting others. 40+ minutes late (and unreachable) counts as a No-Show. We strictly enforce a Blacklist Policy and reserve the right to refuse future service.
⛔
Blacklist Policy
A single no-show (over 40 mins late and unreachable) or last-minute cancellation (within 1 hour) will result in a permanent ban on future bookings. Please treasure each appointment.

Click here to enter the booking system > "會員中心(Member Center)" > "我的紀錄(My Records)" to view your booking status.
Or via our Line account:
  • Go to our official Line account
  • Tap "點我預約" in the lower-left menu to enter our booking system
  • Tap "☰" in the top-left > "我的紀錄(My Records)"

Cancellation Policy

  • Within 24 hours: Contact us via LINE to cancel

To respect the rights of other guests, please contact us as early as possible if you cannot attend or need to change your plans.
⛔
Blacklist Policy
